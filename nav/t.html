Basic Controls

Here’s how you can interact with your world in *Multicreate*:

  • Right Click: Place blocks. Right-click to place blocks where you want to build.
  • Middle click: to interact with some blocks press middle click like tnt , air (if theres 1 lilly and 1 bob) "B" .
  • Left Click: Delete blocks. Left-click to remove any blocks you want to get rid of.
  • C: Open the menu. Press "C" to open the block menu and choose blocks to place.
  • S: Open the menu. Press "S" to open the mob menu and choose mob to spawn.
  • V: Save your world. Press "V" to save your progress and keep your current world.
  • B: Load your world. Press "B" to load a previously saved world and continue where you left off.
  • T: if you HATE the textures you can have a texture pack
